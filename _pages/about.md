He is currently a Professor in [National Laboratory of Pattern Recognition (NLPR)](http://www.nlpr.ia.ac.cn/), [Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences](http://www.ia.ac.cn/). He obtained his Ph.D. in Computer Science from NLPR in 2010. His advisor is Prof. [Jun Zhao](http://www.nlpr.ia.ac.cn/cip/jzhao.htm).

In 2005, he obtained his M.S. in Pattern Recognition from the [Institute of Intelligent Information Processing](http://see.xidian.edu.cn/iiip/), [Xidian University](http://www.xidian.edu.cn/), under the supervision of Prof. [Licheng Jiao](http://see.xidian.edu.cn/iiip/article_show2.asp?ArticleID=1147). Prior to that, he obtained his B.E. from the [School of Electronic Engineering](http://see.xidian.edu.cn/defaulte.asp), [Xidian University](http://www.xidian.edu.cn/). He was a visiting scholar in the [Baidu](http://www.baidu.com) NLP group from 9/2010 to 12/2010 (working with Prof. [Haifeng Wang](http://ir.hit.edu.cn/~wanghaifeng/)).

His research interests include **Information Extraction**, **Question Answering**, **Knowledge Graph**, **Natural Language Processing** and **Neural Symbolic Learning**. He has published more than 80 papers at top-tier conferences and journals including TKDE, ACL, IJCAI, EMNLP, WWW, CIKM, COLING, etc. He was the recipient of the COLING 2014 Best Paper Award, the Google Focused Research Award in 2015 and 2016, the "CCF-Tencent" Research Excellent Award in 2014, the "Hanwang Youth Innovation Excellence Award" in 2014 by the Chinese Information Processing Society, the first prize of the "QianWeiChang Chinese Information Processing Scientific and Technological Progress Award" by the Chinese Information Processing Society in 2018, and the first prize of the "Beijing Scientific and Technological Progress Awards" in 2019.

He is currently a Youth Scientist of the [Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence](https://www.baai.ac.cn), and a Professor of the [University of Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://www.ucas.ac.cn/). He served as the Chair of the Youth Working Committee (2018-2020) in the Chinese Information Processing Society, General Secretary of the Special Group of Language and Knowledge Computing (2017-present) in the Chinese Information Processing Society, and Associate Editor of Pattern Recognition. He has also served for many AI conferences, such as General Chairs (CCKS-2022), PC Chairs (CCKS-2020, CCL-2021), and SAC/SPC/AC (ACL (2017, 2019), EMNLP (2021, 2022), AAAI (2022), COLING (2022), ISWC (2020), EACL (2020), AACL (2020), CIKM (2020, 2021), NLPCC (2022)).
